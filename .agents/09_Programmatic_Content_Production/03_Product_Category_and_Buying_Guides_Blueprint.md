## 1. Product & Category Production Framework

### E-Commerce Category Page Production Template (`/products/[category]`)
Every category page (Dry Fruits, Gift Boxes, Corporate Hampers, Chocolates) requires:
1. **Header Copy (250 words):** Contextual overview highlighting FSSAI certification and nitrogen-packed freshness.
2. **Product Grid:** Responsive 4-column product grid with quick-view, price, rating, and "Add to Cart / Request Quote" buttons.
3. **Category Buying Guide (600 words):** Explaining nut grading, gift packaging options, and bulk discount structures.
4. **FAQ Accordion (4-6 questions):** Integrated with `FAQPage` schema.
