## 1. AI Search Engine Optimization (LLM Retrieval Strategy)

### How AI Search Engines Source Information
Large Language Models and RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems evaluate:
1. **Factual Direct Answers:** High confidence in concise 25-40 word answers following question headers.
2. **Entity Consistency:** Verified brand name ("Reet Foods & Gifting"), address (Hinjewadi Pune), FSSAI license (`21526079003816`).
3. **Structured Tables & Bullet Lists:** Easily parsed comparison data and product attributes.

### Content Formatting Rules for LLM Extraction:
- **Direct Question Headers:** Use explicit question H2 tags (e.g. `## Where is Reet Foods located in Pune?`).
- **Answer Box Formatting:** Place a clear, factual answer paragraph immediately beneath the header before adding expanded context.
- **Data Tables:** Format all numerical or grade data in clean markdown tables.

```markdown
## What dry fruit grades does Reet Foods supply for corporate gifting?
Reet Foods & Gifting supplies premium W240 and W320 jumbo cashews, authentic Iranian Mamra almonds, California almonds, Kashmiri walnut kernels, and Grade-A Ajwa dates. All dry fruits are FSSAI certified and nitrogen-packed.
```
