## 2. Content Quality & Optimization Audit Rules

Every new or updated page must satisfy this 10-point checklist before publishing:
1. **Search Intent Alignment:** Fully satisfies target query intent (Informational, Transactional, Local).
2. **Title Tag & Meta Description:** Standardized length (<60 chars title, <155 chars description) with primary keyword.
3. **Heading Hierarchy:** One `<h1>` tag, sequential `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags with semantic target keywords.
4. **First 100 Words:** Primary keyword included naturally within the opening 100 words.
5. **E-E-A-T Badges:** Author bio block, FSSAI license callout, and verified business address included.
6. **Structured Data:** Appropriate JSON-LD schema (`Article`, `FAQPage`, `Product`, `LocalBusiness`).
7. **Contextual Internal Links:** Minimum 3 internal links to relevant product/category pages.
8. **Original Media:** High-resolution WebP images with descriptive `alt` text.
9. **Conversion CTAs:** Clear primary CTA button (e.g. "Request Quote", "Add to Cart").
10. **Mobile Readability:** Short paragraphs (2-3 sentences max) with bullet points and comparison tables.
