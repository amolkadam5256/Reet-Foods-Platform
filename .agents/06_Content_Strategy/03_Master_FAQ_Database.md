## 2. Product Quality & Nut Grading FAQs (76–150)

76. **What does W240 grade cashew mean?**  
    _Answer:_ W240 refers to Whole White Cashews with approximately 240 nuts per pound. It represents jumbo-grade, large, creamy cashews.

77. **What is the difference between Mamra almonds and California almonds?**  
    _Answer:_ Mamra almonds are grown in Iran/Afghanistan, contain up to 50% natural almond oil, and offer higher nutrition, while California almonds are larger and milder in oil content.

78. **What is nitrogen-flushed packaging?**  
    _Answer:_ Nitrogen flushing replaces oxygen inside the box/pouch with food-grade nitrogen gas, preventing oxidation, rancidity, and retaining 100% crunchiness without preservatives.

79. **How long do Reet Foods dry fruit gift boxes stay fresh?**  
    _Answer:_ Vacuum sealed and nitrogen flushed dry fruits have a shelf life of 6 to 9 months when stored in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

80. **Are your roasted cashews and almonds fried or dry roasted?**  
    _Answer:_ Our flavored cashews and almonds are 100% dry roasted without palm oil, retaining healthy natural nut oils.
