---

## 1. Universal Keyword Placement Rules

To maximize Google NLP and LSI scoring, target keywords must be placed systematically:

| On-Page Element | Primary Keyword | Secondary Keywords | Semantic & LSI Keywords |
|-----------------|-----------------|--------------------|-------------------------|
| **URL Slug** | Exact match in slug | ❌ Avoid stuffing | ❌ Avoid stuffing |
| **Title Tag** | First 30 characters | Include secondary | Brand Suffix at end |
| **Meta Description** | Near beginning | Include 1-2 secondary | Natural phrasing |
| **H1 Tag** | Exact or near-exact | ❌ Avoid stuffing | Natural semantic flow |
| **Opening 100 Words** | Mention within 1st paragraph | 1 secondary keyword | Establish entity context |
| **H2 Subheadings** | Include in key H2s | Spread across H2s | Use question formats |
| **Image Alt Text** | Describe image + kw | Use variant keywords | ❌ Avoid over-optimization |
| **Schema JSON-LD** | Insert in `name` & `description` | In `knowsAbout` / `keywords` | Add entity IDs |
