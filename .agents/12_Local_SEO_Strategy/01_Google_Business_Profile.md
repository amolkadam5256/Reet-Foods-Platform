## STEP 6 — GBP FAQs (10 Questions)

1. **What is the minimum order for corporate gifts?**
   - Minimum order is 50 units for bulk/corporate packages. Contact us for a custom quote.

2. **Do you offer same‑day delivery in Pune?**
   - Yes, same‑day delivery is available in Pune for orders placed before 12 PM.

3. **Can I add a custom logo to the gift packaging?**
   - Yes, we offer custom branded packaging with your company logo on all orders.

4. **What types of corporate gifts do you offer?**
   - We offer dry fruit hampers, chocolate boxes, luxury gift boxes, custom hampers, and more.

5. **Do you ship PAN‑India?**
   - Yes, we ship across all major Indian cities with delivery in 2–5 business days.

6. **How do I place a bulk order?**
   - Visit reetfoodsngiftings.com/bulk-orders/ or call us for pricing.

7. **What is the price range for corporate gift boxes?**
   - Our corporate gifts start at ₹299 and go up to ₹9,999+ per unit.

8. **Do you offer Diwali gift hampers?**
   - Yes, our Diwali hampers are available from August onwards. Pre‑order recommended.

9. **Can I customize the contents of a gift hamper?**
   - Yes, fully customizable hampers are available for orders of 50+ units.

10. **What payment methods are accepted?**
    - We accept UPI, NEFT, RTGS, credit/debit cards, and corporate purchase orders.
