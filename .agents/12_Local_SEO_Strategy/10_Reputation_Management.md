# Reputation Management Strategy — Reet Foods & Giftings

---

## Goal
Protect, enhance, and proactively manage the online reputation of Reet Foods & Giftings across all digital channels, ensuring consistent 4.8+ ratings and fast resolution of negative feedback.

## SECTION 2 — Review Response SOP

| Review Type | Response Time | Action |
|------------|--------------|--------|
| 5-star positive | Within 12 hours | Thank + encourage repeat |
| 4-star | Within 12 hours | Thank + address minor issue |
| 3-star neutral | Within 6 hours | Thank + ask for specific feedback + offer resolution |
| 2-star negative | Within 4 hours | Apologize + move to private resolution |
| 1-star critical | Within 2 hours | Escalate to manager + public apology + private offer |

---

## SECTION 3 — Negative Review Mitigation

### Workflow
1. **Receive alert** via GBP notification.
2. **Assess:** Is the review genuine or spam/fake?
   - If spam → Report to Google via GBP dashboard.
   - If genuine → Proceed to step 3.
3. **Post public response** within 2 hours (from templates in 09_Review_Strategy.md).
4. **Private outreach:** Call the customer at +91 9890609611 or email reetfoodspune@gmail.com with escalation offer.
5. **Resolve:** Offer replacement, refund, or discount on next order.
6. **Request update:** After resolution, politely ask the customer to update or remove the review.
7. **Log** in the incident log below.

### Escalation Matrix
| Severity | Trigger | Escalation |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Low | 3-star, minor complaint | CS Team handles |
| Medium | 2-star, delivery or quality issue | CS Team + Manager |
| High | 1-star, public complaint, social media | MD/Owner involved |
| Crisis | Viral negative post, media mention | PR response plan |

## SECTION 4 — Proactive Reputation Building

### Monthly Actions
- Publish 2 customer success stories on website and social media.
- Share positive reviews as social media posts (with customer permission).
- Add a "What Our Clients Say" section to homepage and city pages.
- Feature 1 detailed case study per month on the blog.

### Partner Reviews
- Request testimonials from corporate clients after major gifting campaigns.
- Feature partner logos (with permission) on the website.

|---------|---------|
