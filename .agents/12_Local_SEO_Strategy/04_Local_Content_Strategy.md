## SECTION 5 — Content SOP

1. **Brief:** Assign keyword, city, occasion, pillar.
2. **Research:** SERP Top 5, People Also Ask, competitor analysis.
3. **Draft:** 1,500–3,000 words (city guides), 800–1,200 words (blogs).
4. **Optimize:** Primary keyword in title, H1, first 100 words, one subheading, meta.
5. **Schema:** FAQPage or Article schema per 07_Local_Schema.md.
6. **Internal Links:** 5–8 links to relevant product/hub/city pages.
7. **CTA:** Primary to /contact/ + secondary to product page.
8. **Review:** SEO → Editorial → Brand.
9. **Publish:** Deploy, submit sitemap, ping GSC.
10. **Track:** Monitor rankings weekly; refresh if drops >5 positions.
