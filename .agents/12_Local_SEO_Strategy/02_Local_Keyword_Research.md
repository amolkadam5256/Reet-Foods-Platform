## Methodology
- **Seed Terms:** Product and service names + location modifiers.
- **Tools:** Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs, SEMrush, AnswerThePublic, Google Trends.
- **Filters:** Volume >10/mo, KD <50, Commercial or Transactional intent.
- **Grouping:** By City, Service, Product, Occasion, Industry.
