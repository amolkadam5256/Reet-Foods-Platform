## 1. 90-Day Keyword Implementation Roadmap

```
Phase 1: Foundation & Geo Landing Pages (Days 1–30)
  ├── 1. Optimize Meta Titles & Headings for core 7 routes
  ├── 2. Launch `/corporate-gifting-pune` & `/corporate-gifting-hinjewadi`
  ├── 3. Implement Schema markup (Organization, Service, Product)
  └── 4. Claim and optimize Google Business Profile (Hinjewadi, Pune)

Phase 2: E-Commerce Product & Category SEO (Days 31–60)
  ├── 1. Optimize product titles & descriptions for Cashews, Almonds, Pistachios
  ├── 2. Launch `/corporate-gifting/under-500` & `/corporate-gifting/under-1000`
  ├── 3. Publish 10 core blog articles targeting top-of-funnel questions
  └── 4. Build internal link SILOs connecting products to corporate landing pages

Phase 3: AI Search, Long-Tail & Content Scaling (Days 61–90)
  ├── 1. Publish 20 blog articles from Category A & Category B keyword clusters
  ├── 2. Implement FAQSchema & SpeakableSchema across all landing pages
  ├── 3. Launch `/corporate-diwali-gifts` & `/wedding-return-gifts`
  └── 4. Monitor search rankings, GA4 lead conversions, and GSC impressions
```
