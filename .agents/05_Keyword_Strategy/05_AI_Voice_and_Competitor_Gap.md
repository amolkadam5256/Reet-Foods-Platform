## 1. AI Search Engine Optimization (LLM Visibility)

### How AI Search Engines Retrieve Answers:
AI models index pages that feature clear factual content, structured tables, direct Q&A formats, and authoritative entity markers.

### AI Search Keyword Strategies:
1. **Brand Entity Prompt Alignment:** Optimize for queries like *"Where can I buy certified corporate gift hampers in Hinjewadi Pune?"*
2. **Comparison Table Extraction:** Publish comparison tables comparing dry fruit grades (W240 vs W320 cashews, Mamra vs California almonds) so LLMs cite Reet Foods as the reference source.
3. **Structured Q&A Blocks:** Include short, 2-3 sentence answers directly under `<h2>` headings for conversational prompts.

```markdown
## What is the minimum order quantity for corporate gifts at Reet Foods?
Reet Foods & Gifting accommodates bulk corporate orders starting from 25 units up to 1,000+ custom hampers per order. All boxes feature custom branding and FSSAI certified packaging.
```
