## 1. Business Profile & Positioning

### Business Model

Reet Foods & Gifting operates a hybrid **B2B Corporate Gifting & Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) Retail** business model specializing in premium dry fruits, artisanal chocolates, and customized gift hampers.

### Product Portfolio

1. **Premium Dry Fruits & Nuts:** Cashews (W240, W320, Flavored/Roasted), Almonds (Mamra, California), Pistachios, Walnuts, Raisins, Dates.
2. **Artisanal Chocolates:** Handcrafted chocolate boxes, custom branded bars.
3. **Corporate Gift Boxes & Hampers:** Customized luxury boxes with corporate logo branding, employee welcome kits, client appreciation gifts, festive (Diwali, New Year) hampers.
4. **Wedding & Event Return Gifts:** Premium packaging dry fruit boxes and hampers for weddings, anniversaries, and family celebrations.

### Target Audiences & Personas

- **Corporate HR & Procurement Managers (B2B):** Seeking reliable, bulk-fulfilled, FSSAI-certified gift hampers with custom logo branding and tax/GST compliance.
- **Festival & Event Buyers (B2C/B2B):** Individuals and companies purchasing Diwali, New Year, Rakhi, and Eid gift boxes.
- **Wedding Planners & Hosts (B2C):** Families looking for elegant, high-end return gifts in custom tin or wooden boxes.
- **Health-Conscious Premium Consumers (D2C):** Buyers seeking nitrogen-packed, fresh, premium-grade nuts and dry fruits for personal consumption.

### Primary Differentiators & Value Proposition

- **FSSAI Certified Freshness:** Certified food production (`21525083009881`) ensuring strict safety and hygiene.
- **Nitrogen-Packed Packaging:** Retains aroma, crunch, and nutritional integrity.
- **End-to-End Corporate Customization:** In-house logo printing, custom greeting cards, bespoke hamper composition.
- **High-Capacity Fulfillment:** Capable of delivering up to 500+ gift boxes per day with PAN India shipping.
