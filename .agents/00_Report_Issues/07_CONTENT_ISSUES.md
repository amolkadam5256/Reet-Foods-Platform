# Overview

Content is one of the strongest ranking signals used by Google and AI-powered search engines.

This document evaluates the quality, relevance, uniqueness, topical authority, readability, and search intent alignment of the website's content.

The objective is to build content that satisfies:

- Google Helpful Content System
- EEAT (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)
- AI Search Engines
- Large Language Models (LLMs)
- User Intent
- Semantic Search

# CNT-001 — Improve Content Readability

## Current Status

Content is informative but contains long paragraphs and dense sections.

---

## Why It Matters

Readable content:

- Improves engagement
- Reduces bounce rate
- Helps AI understand content
- Improves featured snippet eligibility

---

## Recommended Actions

- Break large paragraphs
- Use shorter sentences
- Add bullet points
- Add comparison tables
- Add visual hierarchy

---

# CNT-002 — Improve Keyword Distribution

## Current Status

Primary keywords are not consistently distributed across:

- Introduction
- Headings
- Body
- Conclusion

---

## Recommendation

Use

- Primary Keyword
- Secondary Keywords
- Semantic Keywords
- Related Entities

naturally throughout the content.

---

# CNT-003 — Expand Topical Coverage

## Goal

Cover every aspect of the topic.

Example:

For a recruitment article include

- Overview
- Vacancy Details
- Eligibility
- Age Limit
- Selection Process
- Salary
- Important Dates
- Exam Pattern
- Syllabus
- FAQs
- Official Links

---

## Benefit

Build topical authority and improve long-tail rankings.

---

# CNT-004 — Strengthen EEAT

## Current Status

EEAT signals are limited.

---

## Recommended Actions

- Display author name
- Add author bio
- Mention review date
- Include editorial policy
- Add citations where applicable
- Show source references

---

## Goal

Increase trust and credibility.

---

# CNT-005 — Improve FAQ Coverage

## Current Status

Limited FAQ content.

---

## Recommendation

- Eligibility

---

## Benefit

# CNT-006 — Improve Internal Content Linking

# CNT-007 — Content Freshness

## Current Status

Content should be reviewed regularly.

---

## Recommendation

# CNT-008 — AI-Friendly Content

## Goal

Make content easy for AI systems to interpret.

---

## Best Practices

- Clear headings
- Short paragraphs
- Direct answers
- Structured lists
- Tables where appropriate
- FAQ sections
- Consistent terminology
