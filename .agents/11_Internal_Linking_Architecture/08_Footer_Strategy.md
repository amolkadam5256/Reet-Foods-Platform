# 08 — Footer Strategy

**Website:** https://reetfoodsngiftings.com
**Date:** August 2026

---

## Footer SEO Role

The footer appears on every page of the website, making it one of the most powerful internal linking tools. Every link in the footer receives a vote from all pages simultaneously.

Footer strategy:
- **Distribute authority to key pages** that need more crawl equity
- **Ensure all important pages are reachable** from every page
- **Include conversion links** (Contact, Quote, WhatsApp)
- **Support local SEO** via city landing page links
- **Provide policy links** without polluting primary navigation

## Footer SEO Rules

1. **All footer links must be HTML `<a>` tags** — not JavaScript
2. **No `rel="nofollow"` on footer internal links** — they are important
3. **Footer must render on server-side** — not purely client-side JS
4. **Maximum 50 links in footer** — beyond this, authority is diluted per link
5. **Policy links go in footer ONLY** — remove from header navigation
6. **City pages go in footer** — this is their primary sitewide link
7. **Contact CTA must be visible** — separate styled button, not just text link
8. **Footer must include structured data** — `LocalBusiness` schema or Organization

## Footer Design Specifications

### Visual Hierarchy
- Background: Dark (#1a1a1a or brand dark)
- Text: Light gray (#cccccc)
- Links: White, hover underline
- CTA Button: Brand accent color (orange/gold)
- Dividers: Subtle #333 border

### Mobile Footer
- Collapsed accordion sections
- Each column collapsible with + / - icon
- Contact CTA always visible (not collapsed)
- Social icons prominent
