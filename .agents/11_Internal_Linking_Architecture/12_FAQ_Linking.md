## FAQ Linking Strategy

FAQ pages are underutilized SEO assets. When optimized with proper internal linking, FAQ pages:
- Capture conversational and voice search queries
- Feed Google's "People Also Ask" boxes
- Feed AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) with direct answers
- Create pathways from informational intent to conversion
- Distribute authority from high-traffic questions to product/service pages

### FAQ Linking Rule: MINIMUM 5 INTERNAL LINKS per FAQ page

Every FAQ answer must contain at least one internal link that either:
1. Points to a relevant product or category
2. Points to a guide or resource
3. Points to a conversion/contact page
