## What is Contextual Linking?

Contextual links appear naturally within the body content of a page. They are:
- Surrounded by relevant, topically related text
- Using descriptive, keyword-rich anchor text
- Placed where they add value to the reader
- Pointing to pages that expand on the linked topic

Google's NLP systems evaluate the surrounding text context to understand link relevance. AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) use contextual links to build entity graphs. This guide defines the exact contextual linking strategy for every page type.

---

## Contextual Linking Rules (Global)

1. **Minimum 5 contextual links per page** (excluding nav, breadcrumbs, footer)
2. **Maximum 20 contextual links per page** (avoid over-linking)
3. **Anchor text must describe destination** (not "click here" or "read more")
4. **Links must be relevant** (no forced linking to unrelated pages)
5. **Vary anchor text** (exact match, partial match, branded, entity)
6. **Link early in content** (first 200 words should contain 1–2 links)
7. **Link to conversion pages** (at least 1 contextual link to a CTA page)

## Contextual Linking — Product Pages (Template)

For every product page, include contextual links to:

| Link Type | Target | Anchor Example |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Parent category | /[category]/ | "part of our [Category] collection" |
| Related product 1 | /products/[related-1]/ | "[Product Name]" |
| Related product 2 | /products/[related-2]/ | "[Product Name]" |
| Related product 3 | /products/[related-3]/ | "[Product Name]" |
| Corporate use | /corporate-gifts/ | "ideal for corporate gifting" |
| Bulk orders | /bulk-orders/ | "order in bulk for events" |
| Supporting blog | /blog/[relevant]/ | "read our [Topic] guide" |
| FAQ | /faq/[topic]/ | "see [Topic] FAQ" |
| Contact/quote | /contact/ | "request a custom quote" |

## Contextual Linking — Blog Pages (Template)

For every blog article, include contextual links to:

| Link Type | Target | Anchor Example | Position |
|-----------|--------|----------------|----------|
| Hub page | /[hub]/ | "[Topic] collection" | First 200 words |
| Product 1 | /products/[x]/ | "[Product Name]" | Within content |
| Product 2 | /products/[y]/ | "[Product Name]" | Within content |
| Category | /[category]/ | "[Category] page" | Within content |
| Related blog | /blog/[related]/ | "our guide to [Topic]" | Mid-content |
| FAQ | /faq/[topic]/ | "[Topic] FAQ" | Toward end |
| Corporate page | /corporate-gifts/ | "corporate gifting solutions" | If B2B relevant |
| CTA page | /contact/ | "request a free quote" | Final paragraph |

**Example — Blog: "Best Corporate Gifts India":**

```

can strengthen client relationships and boost employee morale..."

[Mixed Nut Corporate Box](/products/mixed-nut-corporate-box/), remain top sellers..."

Closing: "...For bulk requirements, explore our [Bulk Gifting Solutions](/bulk-gifting-solutions/)
or [contact us](/contact/) for a customized quote."
```
