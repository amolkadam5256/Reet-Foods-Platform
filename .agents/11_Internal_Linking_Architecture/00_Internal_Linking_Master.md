## Executive Summary

Reet Foods & Giftings operates a premium gifting and food business serving corporate clients, retail consumers, and bulk buyers across India. The website currently lacks a structured internal linking architecture, resulting in:

- Orphan pages receiving no crawl equity
- Authority concentrated at top-level pages without flowing down
- Product and blog pages disconnected from conversion paths
- No topical clusters established for AI search visibility
- Click depth exceeding 4+ for key commercial pages

This document defines the complete enterprise internal linking system to:

1. **Maximize crawlability** — Every page discoverable within 3 clicks
2. **Distribute PageRank** — Systematic authority flow from Tier 1 to Tier 4
3. **Build topical authority** — 10 complete topic clusters with hub & spoke architecture
4. **Support AI search** — Entity-first linking for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity
5. **Increase conversions** — Every content page linked to relevant commercial pages
6. **Eliminate orphan pages** — 100% page coverage in linking architecture
