## Overview

Topical clusters establish semantic authority by grouping all related content — hub pages, supporting pages, blog articles, buying guides, FAQs, products, and resources — around a central topic.

Google, Bing, and AI search engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) reward websites that demonstrate comprehensive topical coverage. Each cluster below is designed to:

- Signal full topical authority to search engines
- Guide users through the complete purchase journey
- Feed AI knowledge graphs with structured entity relationships
- Distribute PageRank efficiently within topic domains
