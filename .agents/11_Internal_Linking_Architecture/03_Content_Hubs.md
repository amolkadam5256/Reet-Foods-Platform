## What is a Content Hub?

A Content Hub is a comprehensive, authority-building page that serves as the definitive resource for a topic. It aggregates:
- Introduction and overview
- Subtopic sections
- Featured products
- Blog articles
- Buying guides
- FAQs
- Downloadable resources
- Case studies / testimonials

Content hubs signal topical authority to Google, enable AI search engines to extract structured answers, and serve as the central PageRank distribution point for each topic cluster.
