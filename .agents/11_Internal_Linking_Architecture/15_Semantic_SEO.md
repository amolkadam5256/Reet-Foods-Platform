## Semantic SEO Overview

Semantic SEO goes beyond keyword matching to establish meaning, context, and topic relationships. Modern search engines (Google, Bing, and AI search engines) use semantic understanding to:

- Identify what a page is truly about
- Understand entity relationships
- Determine topical authority and coverage gaps
- Extract information for AI Overviews, featured snippets, and knowledge panels
- Rank based on semantic relevance, not just keyword density

This blueprint defines the semantic optimization strategy for Reet Foods & Giftings.

### Pillar 5 — Semantic Internal Linking (Context Windows)

For AI search engines, the 50–100 words surrounding an internal link define the link's semantic meaning. Optimize the context window:

```
❌ POOR SEMANTIC SIGNAL:
"Click here to see our gift boxes."
→ "Click here" tells AI nothing about the destination

✅ STRONG SEMANTIC SIGNAL:
"For premium corporate gifting, our [luxury gift hampers](/gift-boxes/) 
contain hand-selected dry fruits from certified farms, packaged in 
elegant boxes ideal for Diwali, client appreciation, and employee recognition."
→ AI understands: gift hampers = luxury, corporate, dry fruits, Diwali, B2B
```

### Pillar 7 — Content Silos (Semantic Isolation)

Content silos prevent topic dilution. Each cluster must:
- Have clear thematic focus
- Link primarily within its own topic cluster
- Cross-link to other clusters only when semantically justified

```
CORPORATE GIFTING SILO:
/corporate-gifts/
/employee-gifting-programs/
/client-gifting-solutions/
/bulk-gifting-solutions/
/corporate-gifting-pune/
/blog/best-corporate-gifts-india/
/faq/corporate-gifting/
→ These pages link heavily to each other
→ They link to DRY FRUITS only when contextually relevant ("filled with dry fruits")
→ They do NOT randomly link to cold pressed juices (different topic)
```
