# Comprehensive Call-To-Action (CTA) Strategy — Reet Foods & Giftings

---

## SECTION 1 — Master CTA Hierarchy

| CTA Class                   | Primary Purpose                    | Copy Standard                       | Visual Style / Color                      | Placement                          |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| **P1 - Corporate Quote**    | Capture high-ticket B2B bulk leads | `Request Corporate Bulk Quote`      | Royal Gold (`#D4AF37`) Button, Bold Text  | Hero, City Pages, Product Buy-Box  |
| **P1 - Direct Purchase**    | Drive e-commerce sales             | `Add to Cart` / `Buy Now`           | Deep Emerald (`#2C5E3B`) Button           | Product Detail Pages               |
| **P1 - WhatsApp Lead**      | Instant chat conversion            | `Chat on WhatsApp`                  | WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`)                | Mobile Sticky Bar, Product Buy-Box |
| **P1 - Phone Call Lead**    | Immediate local phone inquiries    | `Call +91 9225130732`               | Dark Charcoal (`#1F2937`) with Phone Icon | Header Top Bar, Contact Page       |
| **P2 - Catalogue Download** | Mid-funnel lead capture            | `Download 2025 Corporate Catalogue` | Outlined Gold Button with PDF Icon        | B2B Pages, Footer                  |
| **P3 - Secondary Offer**    | Exit intent / retargeting capture  | `Get 5% Pre-Order Discount`         | Text Link / Accent Button                 | Exit Modal, Banner                 |

---

## SECTION 2 — Contextual CTA Rules

- **Mobile Viewports:** Always display the sticky bottom bar with `[ 💬 WhatsApp Quote ]` and `[ 📞 Call Store ]`.
- **Hinjewadi & City Pages:** CTA copy must include city context: _"Request Bulk Quote for Hinjewadi IT Companies"_.
- **Peak Festival Season (Aug–Nov):** Update CTAs with urgency micro-copy: _"Pre-Order Diwali Hampers — Guaranteed Delivery"_.
