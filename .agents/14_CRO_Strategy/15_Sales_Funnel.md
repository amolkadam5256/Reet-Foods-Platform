# End-to-End Sales Funnel Optimization — Reet Foods & Giftings

---

## SECTION 1 — Funnel Mapping & Drop-off Recovery

Reet Foods tracks and recovers drop-offs across 3 primary sales funnels.

```
                  [ B2B CORPORATE FUNNEL ]
 Visitor Lands (/corporate-gifts/hinjewadi/ or /bulk-orders/)
                          │  (100%)
                          ▼
 Clicks "Request Corporate Quote" Modal / CTA
                          │  (25%)
                          ▼
 Starts Form Field Entry (Name, Corporate Email, Phone)
                          │  (80% of starts)
                          ▼
 Completes Form Submission (`generate_lead`)
                          │  (65% completion -> 13% overall)
                          ▼
 Sales Team Outbound WhatsApp / Call (+91 9225130732) within 15 Min
                          │  (90% contact rate)
                          ▼
 Sample Delivery / Digital Proof Approval ──> Final Invoice Payment
```

---

## SECTION 2 — Abandonment Recovery Tactics

1. **Form Abandonment:** If user drops off after filling email, fire retargeting email with catalogue PDF link.
2. **E-commerce Cart Abandonment:** Send WhatsApp cart recovery template within 30 minutes offering free delivery in Pune.
