## SECTION 2 — Monitoring & Reporting Cadence

- **Weekly CRO Diagnostic:** Conducted using Microsoft Clarity session recordings and GA4 funnel drop-off reports.
- **Bi-Weekly A/B Test Review:** Evaluate active experiments for 95% statistical significance ($p < 0.05$).
- **Monthly Executive CRO Summary:** Executive report detailing lead lift, conversion rate gains, and revenue growth.
