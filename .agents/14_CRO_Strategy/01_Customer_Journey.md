# Customer Journey Mapping & Persona Framework — Reet Foods & Giftings

---

## SECTION 1 — Persona Library

### Persona 1: Corporate HR / Admin Manager ("Priya - Hinjewadi IT Lead")
- **Profile:** HR Director at an IT MNC in Hinjewadi Phase I, Pune (500+ employees).
- **Core Goal:** Source 500 premium Diwali gift hampers with custom company logo branding within budget (₹1,000–₹2,500 / unit).
- **Pain Points:** Vendor unreliability, poor packaging quality, lack of GST invoice, delayed delivery during peak festival season.
- **Key Decision Triggers:** FSSAI certification, GST invoice availability, physical store proximity near Hinjewadi, sample availability, quick response on WhatsApp.
- **Preferred CTA:** "Request Corporate Quote" / "Chat on WhatsApp".

### Persona 2: Wedding & Event Planner ("Vikram - Luxury Event Manager")
- **Profile:** Event planner organizing high-end weddings in Pune, Mumbai, and Jaipur.
- **Core Goal:** Source 200 luxury dry fruit gift boxes for wedding return favors.
- **Pain Points:** Generic designs, lack of custom packaging elegance, unverified food safety.
- **Key Decision Triggers:** Premium aesthetics, custom ribbon/branding, handcrafted dry fruits, client reviews.
- **Preferred CTA:** "Download Catalogue" / "Call Store Specialist".

### Persona 3: Individual Festive Buyer ("Ananya - Retail Consumer")
- **Profile:** Resident of Baner, Pune buying gifts for family and friends.
- **Core Goal:** Buy 5 premium dry fruit boxes online with fast same-day delivery in Pune.
- **Pain Points:** High shipping costs, uncertain delivery timelines.
- **Preferred CTA:** "Buy Now" / "Same-Day Pune Delivery".

## SECTION 2 — Multi-Stage Customer Journey Map

```
Stage 1: AWARENESS
Google Search ("corporate gifts hinjewadi") / Meta Ad ──> Lands on City Page / Product Page
                                 │
Stage 2: RESEARCH & EVALUATION
Explores Dry Fruit Boxes / Reads GST & FSSAI Trust Signals / Checks Reviews
                                 │
Stage 3: FRICTION REMOVAL & TRUST
Reviews Client Logo Wall / Clicks WhatsApp for Instant Sample Inquiry
                                 │
Stage 4: INQUIRY & CONVERSION
Submits Quote Form (+91 9890609611) OR Completes Online Checkout
                                 │
Stage 5: RETENTION & REPEAT
Receives Follow-up WhatsApp / Annual Festival Pre-Order Reminder
```

## SECTION 3 — Stage-by-Stage Friction & CRO Solution Matrix

| Journey Stage | Potential User Friction | CRO Optimization Solution | Metric Tracked |
|---------------|------------------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| **Awareness** | Slow page load, unclear value prop | Above-the-fold headline + 3 bullet USPs + speed < 2s | Bounce Rate, LCP |
| **Research** | Unclear bulk pricing & minimums | Display "Bulk Orders from 50 Units | GST Invoices Provided" | Page Scroll Depth |
| **Comparison** | Doubt about food quality & safety | Prominently display FSSAI Licence No. 21526079003816 | Trust Click % |
| **Inquiry** | Long, intimidating form fields | Reduce quote form to 4 fields + add instant WhatsApp button | Form Submit % |
| **Conversion** | Unclear shipping times | Display "Same-Day Delivery in Pune | 2–5 Days PAN-India" | Checkout Start % |
