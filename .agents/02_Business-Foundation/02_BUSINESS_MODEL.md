# Business Model

## Value exchange

SearchSarkariNaukri helps candidates discover timely, relevant government opportunities and reach the official notification or application route. Candidates receive free access to useful, structured information; the business earns revenue without compromising the accuracy or clarity of that journey.

## Proposed model

| Layer | Candidate value | Revenue opportunity | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free job discovery | Current jobs, filters, deadlines, official sources | Display advertising | Ads must not obscure dates, eligibility, or official apply links. |
| Preparation journey | Study material, current affairs, exam guidance | Clearly labelled affiliate referrals | Recommend only relevant, reputable products; disclose commercial relationships. |
| Alerts and retention | Optional job alerts and digests | Audience growth and future sponsorship | Explicit consent, easy unsubscribe, and privacy compliance. |
| Partnerships | Relevant candidate services | Sponsored content or lead generation | Never represent sponsors as government authorities; label paid placement. |

## Primary business metric

**Verified candidate utility:** completed outbound clicks to an official notice or application destination, measured alongside source accuracy and freshness.

## Core unit metrics

- Organic sessions to active job pages.
- Official-notice and apply-link outbound click-through rate.
- Alert opt-in rate and returning-user rate.
- Revenue per thousand sessions, separated by display, affiliate, and sponsorship.
- Percentage of listings with a current, verified official source.
- Time from official update to reviewed publication.

## Commercial guardrails

- The official notice is authoritative; eligibility and selection outcomes must never be guaranteed.
- Editorial priority cannot be purchased in a way that misleads candidates.
- Sponsored and affiliate content must be identified clearly.
- Do not collect sensitive candidate data unless there is a defined lawful purpose, consent path, retention policy, and security control.
