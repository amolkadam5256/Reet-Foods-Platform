# Target Audience

## Priority segments

| Segment | Primary need | Typical discovery query | Best experience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maharashtra local candidate | District and department-specific vacancies | `Pune government jobs`, `MPSC bharti` | District hub, Marathi/English clarity, current deadline status |
| National exam aspirant | Exam-specific updates, syllabus, results, and admit cards | `SSC CGL result`, `UPSC notification` | Exam hub with authoritative links and update history |
| Qualification-led seeker | Jobs matching 10th, 12th, ITI, diploma, or graduate status | `12th pass government jobs` | Qualification-aware filters and plain-language eligibility summary |
| Returning daily checker | Fast update scan and reminders | `today sarkari naukri` | Mobile-fast active-jobs feed and optional alerts |
| Results/admit-card visitor | Direct official destination during high urgency | `[exam] admit card download` | Clear status, official link, steps, and related help |

## Jobs to be done

1. **Help me find a legitimate opportunity I can apply for before the deadline.**
2. **Help me confirm the official source and understand the essential criteria quickly.**
3. **Help me stay updated on my exam without repeatedly searching many websites.**

## Audience constraints

- Mobile-first behaviour and inconsistent connectivity must be assumed.
- Language, state, qualification, reservation/category rules, and deadline urgency materially affect comprehension.
- Candidates may be vulnerable to scams; trust markers and official-source links are product requirements, not decorative content.

## Anti-personas

- Users seeking guaranteed selection, paid application shortcuts, or unofficial certificates.
- Advertisers or partners unwilling to meet clear-labelling and candidate-safety requirements.
