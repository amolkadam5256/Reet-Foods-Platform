# Candidate User Journey

| Stage | Candidate intent | Experience required | Success event | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Find a job, result, or admit card | Relevant search landing page with accurate title and status | Qualified landing-page view | Stale or misleading SERP promise |
| Evaluate | Decide whether it applies to them | Scan-friendly eligibility, dates, location, qualification, and source | Detail-page engagement | Missing critical fields or unclear language |
| Verify | Confirm legitimacy | Official notification/application link plus source context | Official-notice click | Scam confusion or broken outbound link |
| Act | Apply or retrieve an official document | Prominent, clearly external official CTA | Apply-link outbound click | Ad/CTA confusion or mobile friction |
| Return | Monitor updates and prepare | Related jobs, alerts with consent, calendar, results, study content | Return visit or alert opt-in | Unwanted notifications or irrelevant recommendations |

## Journey rules

- Show status and deadline before monetization modules.
- Label external official links clearly; test them routinely.
- If an application is closed, provide the status and useful active alternatives.
- Avoid making alert signup a barrier to viewing critical information.
- Track the funnel without collecting more personal data than necessary.
